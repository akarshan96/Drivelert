Privacy Policy for Drivelert

Drivelert is a driver safety application designed to detect signs of drowsiness using real-time camera analysis.

Information Collection

Drivelert uses the device camera to analyze driver alertness in real time. Camera data is processed locally on the device and is not stored or transmitted to external servers.

Data Usage

The app does not collect personal information, does not store images, and does not share any user data with third parties.

Permissions

The app requires camera access to perform drowsiness detection. This permission is used solely for the core functionality of the app.

Security

All processing occurs on-device, ensuring user privacy and data security.

Children's Privacy

Drivelert is not directed toward children under 13.

This policy may be updated periodically.
